    Guardrails

    • If an MBS at-risk call clashes with a UOM block, the MBS call wins and the UOM block moves.
    • Check whether a UOM window overlaps an MBS census or re-enrolment period. If it does, bring the UOM emails forward and plan cover.
    • UOM work is logged separately in the CRM, so both partners' KPIs stay clean.
    • No UOM cold calls in assessment week, or on assessment due days. Callbacks and vcita bookings only.
    • Review the split with Carms after the first UOM cycle.
    • If she's on leave during a peak, the emails still go out on schedule and a cover advisor takes the call rounds.

    Student success for UOM sits with the UOM student success advisers. We own re-enrolment, end to end. This page is the line between the two.

    We handle

    • Re-enrolment reminder emails at each touchpoint
    • Re-enrolment calls to students who haven't enrolled yet
    • Walking students through the enrolment steps and key dates
    • Booking follow-up calls through vcita
    • Win-back emails to students who lapsed, pointing them to the next intake
    • Logging every attempt and outcome, and reporting the numbers

    We refer to the UOM SSAs

    • Wellbeing, health or personal circumstances affecting study
    • Academic performance, failed subjects or progression concerns
    • Extensions, special consideration or leave of absence
    • Course changes, credit or subject selection advice
    • Complaints or anything the student asks to escalate

    How to refer, in 3 steps

    1. Tell the student who will contact them and roughly when. Don't promise an outcome.
    2. Send the referral using the handover template (Scripts & Templates → Referrals) to [UOM SSA inbox] on the same day.
    3. Log it in the tracker as Referred to UOM SSA, then continue with re-enrolment only if the student wants to.
    Confirm before launch: that re-enrolment contact is within scope of the UOM agreement, and which inbox or contact the UOM SSAs want referrals sent to.

    Measures

    • Re-enrolment rate: re-enrolled ÷ eligible. Compare against the baseline from the last period before this program.
    • Contact rate: reached ÷ eligible. Sits alongside her existing contact-rate KPI.
    • Converted after contact: re-enrolled after a call ÷ reached.
    • Revenue retained: extra re-enrolments × average subject fee.
    • Top reasons for not enrolling: feeds back into the scripts and objection handling.
